=== Observations ===
1. Pre-sized output vectors are faster than back_inserter
2. Simple loops and std::transform with presizing perform similarly
3. Modern compilers auto-vectorize these loops effectively
4. Conversion is typically NOT the bottleneck compared to disk I/O
